## Without Decomposition

User: 
Create the best Pokémon team for a new trainer playing Gen 1 Pokemon Red. Choose six Pokémon, explain each choice, assign each one a role, identify the team’s weaknesses, and suggest a battle strategy.

## With Decomposition (Step by Step)

System: 
Create the best Pokémon team for a new trainer playing Gen 1 Pokemon Red.

Call 1: 
Choose six Pokémon that would make a balanced team for a new trainer.

Call 2: 
Explain the role of each Pokémon on the team.

Call 3: 
Analyze the team for major type weaknesses or coverage gaps.

Call 4: 
Suggest changes that would improve the team.

Call 5: 
Create a simple battle strategy for the revised team.

Final call: 
Combine everything into one clear beginner-friendly team guide.
